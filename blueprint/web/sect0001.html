1 Jordan Form

The first formal object is a predicate saying that a square matrix already has Jordan block shape. At this stage we are not proving that every linear map has such a matrix representation; we are only formalizing what the target shape means.

  • JF.exists_basis_matrix_is_jordan_form_of_minpoly_splits

Every endomorphism of a finite-dimensional vector space whose minimal polynomial splits admits a basis whose matrix representation is in Jordan form.

Proof ▶

Put \(R=K[X]\). Give \(V\) the \(R\)-module structure in which \(X\) acts as \(T\); in mathlib this is the module \( \mathrm{Module.AEval'}(T)\). Since \(V\) is finite-dimensional over \(K\), this is a finitely generated \(K[X]\)-module. It is also torsion: the minimal polynomial \(m_T\) is nonzero and satisfies \(m_T(T)=0\), so every vector is annihilated by the nonzero polynomial \(m_T\).

The Lean development has formalized the first reduction. Because \(K[X]\) is a principal ideal domain, the torsion form of the structure theorem gives an \(R\)-linear isomorphism

\[ V \cong \bigoplus _{a\in A} K[X]/(p_a^{e_a}), \]

with each \(p_a\) irreducible. The annihilator of this \(K[X]\)-module is generated by \(m_T\). Hence each \(p_a^{e_a}\), and in particular every \(p_a\) with \(e_a{\gt}0\), divides \(m_T\). Since \(m_T\) splits over \(K\), every such irreducible \(p_a\) is linear, so for some \(\lambda _a\in K\) it is associated to \(X-\lambda _a\). Replacing \(p_a\) by its monic associate does not change the cyclic quotient, and the decomposition may be read as

\[ V \cong \bigoplus _{a\in A} K[X]/((X-\lambda _a)^{e_a}). \]

Discard the zero quotients, which can only occur when \(e_a=0\). On a remaining summand \(K[X]/((X-\lambda )^{e})\), where \(e{\gt}0\), let \(y=X-\lambda \). The residue classes

\[ y^{e-1},\ y^{e-2},\ \ldots ,\ y,\ 1 \]

form a \(K\)-basis. Multiplication by \(X=\lambda +y\) sends \(y^r\) to \(\lambda y^r+y^{r+1}\), with \(y^e=0\). In the displayed reverse order this is exactly a Jordan block with diagonal entry \(\lambda \) and superdiagonal entries \(1\).

The Lean development proves the single-summand matrix computation: on \(K[X]/((X-\lambda )^{n+1})\), scalar action by \(X\) has matrix exactly the Jordan block \(J_{n+1}(\lambda )\) in the transported reversed basis. It also proves the componentwise direct-sum action, transports this formula across the structure-theorem equivalence back to \(V\), and derives the corresponding matrix-entry formula: in the transported cyclic basis, each column has only its diagonal coefficient and possibly the predecessor coefficient in the same summand.

Finally, enumerate the finite summand index set, concatenate the dependent family of finite cyclic fibers with \(\mathrm{finSigmaFinEquiv}\), and reindex the transported cyclic basis by this block order. The matrix-entry formula then directly verifies the three clauses of \(\mathrm{IsJordanForm}\): all off-diagonal and off-superdiagonal entries are zero, superdiagonal entries are zero or one, and a superdiagonal one only connects entries with the same eigenvalue.
